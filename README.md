# Greenfield_Secondary_School_Student_Academic-_Performance_Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses student academic performance at Greenfield Secondary School across three academic terms. 

The objective is to clean, organise, and analyse student results to identify academic trends, evaluate subject performance, monitor student improvement, and identify students who may require additional academic support.

## Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare student academic records for analysis.

- Calculate students' total scores, average scores, grades, and class rankings.

- Compare academic performance across three terms.

- Analyse performance across six subjects.

- Identify the highest-performing students.

- Track individual student improvement between the First and Third Terms.

- Identify students at risk of poor academic performance.

- Develop an executive summary dashboard to support data-driven decisions.

## Tools Used

Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, formulas, calculations, ranking, analysis, and dashboard development.

## Data Preparation and Cleaning

The original dataset contained 726 records. The following cleaning activities were performed:

- Removed 6 duplicate Student ID and Term records.

- Standardised class names and gender entries.

- Corrected student-name formatting and unnecessary spaces.

- Identified and corrected 3 likely data-entry errors in subject scores.

- Converted subject-score columns to numerical data types.

- Retained 12 missing subject scores as blank values.
Final cleaned dataset: 720 records representing 240 unique students.

## Analysis Performed

1. Student Performance Analysis

Calculated total scores, average scores, grades, and class rankings for each student.

2. Class Performance Analysis

Compared the average scores, student population, and pass rates across six classes and three terms.

3. Subject Performance Analysis

Evaluated the performance of students across:

Mathematics

English

Basic Science

Social Studies

Computer Studies

Civic Education

4. Student Improvement Analysis

Compared First Term and Third Term average scores to identify students who improved, declined, or maintained their performance.

5. At-Risk Student Identification

Identified students with Third Term average scores below 50 to highlight those who may require additional academic attention.

## Key Insights

- Best-performing subject: Civic Education, with an overall average of 67.3.

- Lowest-performing subject: Mathematics, with an overall average of 55.4.

- All six subjects recorded an increase in average performance from the First Term to the Third Term.

- Student-level analysis highlighted individual improvement and declining performance.

- Students with Third Term averages below 50 were flagged for further academic support.

## Recommendations

- Strengthen Mathematics teaching and introduce targeted remedial lessons.

- Provide additional academic support for students whose averages are below 50.

- Monitor students' academic progress consistently across terms.

- Encourage collaboration among teachers to identify and adopt effective teaching practices.

- Use periodic performance analysis to support evidence-based academic decisions.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to transform raw academic records into meaningful insights. Through data cleaning, analysis, and dashboard development, the school can better understand student performance, identify learning gaps, monitor improvement, and make informed decisions to support better academic outcomes.
